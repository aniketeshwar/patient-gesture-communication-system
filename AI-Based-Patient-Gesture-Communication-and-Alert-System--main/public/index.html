AI-Based Patient Gesture Communication and Alert System

Patients who cannot speak can call for help with a hand gesture, an eye blink, a facial expression or a head movement. The system checks each signal, then alerts the nurse on the dashboard or on a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi.
